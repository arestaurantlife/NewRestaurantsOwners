Who we are

NewRestaurantsOwners.com ("NRO," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Corporate Shield Hospitality Solutions LLC, a Colorado limited liability company. We provide education, digital products, training tools, and resources for new restaurant owners and managers.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to:

  • NewRestaurantsOwners.com and its subscription platform
  • Our sub-domains and related sites that link to this policy, including training.newrestaurantsowners.com
  • Our digital product store, emails, webinars, live Q&A sessions, and community features (together, the "Services")

It does not apply to third-party websites or services we link to, or to products we sell through third-party marketplaces such as Amazon, which follow their own privacy policies.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Services, deliver what you buy, and support your account.

Information you give us

  • Account and profile: name, email address, password, business or restaurant name, job title, number of locations, and preferred language.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: billing name, billing address, the plan or product you buy, and transaction history. Card details are entered directly with our payment processor; we do not see or store full card numbers (see Section 3).
  • Waitlist and email sign-ups: name and email address when you join the NRO waitlist, download a free resource, or sign up on one of our sub-domains.
  • Community and live sessions: questions, posts, comments, and messages you submit in community areas, webinars, or live Q&A sessions.
  • Business information you choose to enter: figures you type into our calculators, forms, and templates (for example, sales, food cost, or labor numbers).
  • Support and contact: anything you include when you email us or fill out a contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, and dates and times of visits.
  • Usage data: which lessons, videos, downloads, and tools you use, course progress, and quiz results.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 4.

Information from others

  • Your employer or restaurant: if a restaurant owner gives you a training seat, they provide your name and email address to set up your account (see Section 6).
  • Payment processor: confirmation of payment, subscription status, and the last four digits and expiration date of your card.
  • Marketplaces and ad platforms: limited information, such as campaign performance data, when you reach us through an ad or partner link.

We do not ask for, and ask that you not submit, Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, bank account numbers, or health information through the Services.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your identity when you sign in
  • Deliver the subscription content, digital products, downloads, and training you purchase
  • Process payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations, and refunds
  • Track course progress and quiz results, and show them to you and, for training seats, to the restaurant that assigned the seat
  • Run webinars, live Q&A sessions, and community features
  • Send transactional messages, such as receipts, trial reminders, renewal notices, password resets, and service updates
  • Send newsletters, launch updates, and offers about NRO products, where permitted and with an easy way to unsubscribe
  • Answer questions and provide customer support
  • Understand how the Services are used so we can fix problems and improve our content and tools
  • Protect the Services, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We may create aggregated or de-identified information (for example, "average food cost entered by Starter members") that no longer identifies you. We may use that information for any lawful purpose and will not try to re-identify it.

3. Payments, subscriptions, and free trials

Payments on NewRestaurantsOwners.com are processed by Stripe, Inc. Purchases on some of our product sites, such as The Pre-Shift Playbook, are processed by Gumroad, Inc. When you buy a product or start a subscription, you enter your card details directly into the processor's secure checkout. Stripe handles that information under its own privacy policy. Gumroad does the same under its privacy policy. We receive only what we need to manage your purchase, such as your name, billing address, plan, payment status, and the card's brand, last four digits, and expiration date.

Some plans include a free trial that requires a payment card. We keep the card on file with Stripe so your plan can begin when the trial ends, unless you cancel first.

We keep records of purchases, refunds, and subscription changes as required for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes.

Products you buy from us through third-party stores, such as Amazon, are handled by that store under its own privacy policy. We receive only the limited sales information that store chooses to share.

We share personal information only in these situations:

6. Training seats provided by your restaurant

Our staff training courses on training.newrestaurantsowners.com are purchased by restaurant owners, who assign seats to their employees. Training accounts are separate from NRO subscription accounts.

If your restaurant assigned you a seat:

  • Your restaurant provides your name and email address so we can invite you.
  • Your restaurant's owner and authorized managers can see your enrollment, course progress, quiz scores, completion dates, and certificates of completion for their organization.
  • We process this training information on your restaurant's behalf and follow its reasonable instructions. Your restaurant is responsible for its own use of that information and for any notices it owes its employees.
  • If you leave the restaurant, it may remove you from its roster and keep its own training records under its own policies.
  • For questions about how your restaurant uses your training records, contact your restaurant first. You can always contact us at the address in Section 10.

Our courses are internal staff training. A certificate of completion is not a state-issued license or a state-recognized credential.

If you are a restaurant owner, you confirm that you have the right to give us your employees' names and email addresses for training, and that you will tell your employees how you use their training records.

Choices everyone has

  • Marketing email: click "unsubscribe" in any marketing email. We will still send transactional messages, such as receipts and account notices.
  • Cookies and ads: use our cookie banner, your browser settings, or a Global Privacy Control signal (see Section 4).
  • Cancel or close your account: cancel your subscription in your account or contact us to close your account.

Rights under state privacy laws

Depending on where you live, including Colorado, California, Virginia, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information
  • Get your information in a portable format
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights

We offer these rights to all users, whether or not a specific state law applies to us.

How to make a request

Email Support@newrestaurantsowners.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity, usually by confirming the email address on your account, before acting on a request. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that the agent is authorized. We will respond within 45 days, and we may extend that once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice to you.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to an appeal within 45 days. If you are a Colorado resident and are not satisfied with our appeal decision, you may contact the Colorado Attorney General at coag.gov.
