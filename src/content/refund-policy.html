1. Subscriptions and the free trial

Starter free trial. The 7-day Starter trial is free. If you cancel before it ends, you are not charged. If you do not cancel, your first monthly charge is made when the trial ends. Because the trial lets you try the Starter plan at no cost, a first charge after an uncanceled trial is covered only by the 14-day guarantee below.

14-day first-month guarantee. If you are not satisfied, you may request a full refund of your first paid month within 14 days of that first charge. To qualify, all of the following must be true:

  • It is your first NRO subscription. Refunds are limited to one per person, email address, payment card, and business.
  • You request it in writing within 14 calendar days of the first charge, by emailing refund@newrestaurantsowners.com from the email address on your account.
  • Your use during that time was light: you downloaded or opened no more than 3 guides, eBooks, templates, or forms, and did not download The Psychology of Restaurant Theft, The Pre-Shift Playbook, or any full bundle.
  • Your account is in good standing, with no breach of our Terms of Service and no open chargeback.

If you used more than that, you have received the main value of the plan, and the charge is not refundable. When a refund is approved, your subscription ends and your access stops right away.

Renewals. Monthly renewal charges are not refundable, including for months you did not use. To avoid the next charge, cancel before your renewal date.

Cancellations. You can cancel anytime from your account settings or by emailing us at cancel@newrestaurantsowners.com. Cancellation stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the month you already paid for. We do not give partial or prorated refunds for the unused part of a month.

Downgrades and upgrades. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal and are not refunded. Upgrades are charged right away, and the upgrade charge is not refundable.

Enterprise plans. Enterprise plans follow the same 14-day first-month guarantee and the same limits, unless your written Enterprise agreement says otherwise.

2. eBooks, guides, bundles, and other digital products

Digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so all sales are final. By completing your purchase, you agree that you get immediate access and give up any right to cancel once the download is available.

We will help if something is wrong with the file itself. If a file is defective (it will not open, pages are missing, or it is corrupted) or is materially different from its description, email us within 14 days of purchase. We will first send a working replacement. If we cannot fix the problem within 7 days, we will refund that product.

We do not give refunds for:

  • Changing your mind, or buying a product by mistake after downloading it
  • Content you already own through a subscription, bundle, or another purchase. Check your library before buying.
  • Disagreeing with the content's approach or not getting the results you hoped for
  • Problems caused by your device or software when the file works on standard readers

Gumroad purchases. Some product sites, such as The Pre-Shift Playbook, check out through Gumroad. This policy still applies. Email us, not Gumroad, and we will handle any approved refund.

Amazon and other marketplaces. Products bought on Amazon (including Kindle editions) or other marketplaces follow that marketplace's refund rules. Request refunds directly from them.

3. Staff training platform

License fee. The one-time license fee sets up your restaurant's organization, branding, house policies, and roster. It is not refundable once any seat has been assigned to an employee. If you request a refund within 14 days of purchase and no seat has been assigned, we will refund the license fee minus a $50 setup fee.

Seats. A seat is used as soon as it is assigned to an employee, whether or not the employee starts or finishes the course. Assigned seats are not refundable and cannot be moved to another employee or business. This includes seats assigned to employees who later quit or are let go.

Unused seat packs. You may request a refund of a seat pack within 14 days of purchase only if no seat from that pack has been assigned. Partly used packs are not refundable.

Course content. We do not give refunds because an employee did not pass, did not finish, or because a course does not meet a state certification requirement. Our courses are internal training and are not state credentials, as explained in our Terms of Service.

4. How to request a refund

Email refund@newrestaurantsowners.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Refund Request." Include your name, what you bought, the purchase date, and the reason. We reply within 7 business days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method only, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. We cannot refund to a different card, account, or person.

5. Chargebacks and payment disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. Most problems can be fixed quickly.

If you file a chargeback for a purchase that this policy does not cover:

  • We will respond to the dispute and give your bank our records, including your acceptance of our Terms and this policy at checkout, your account activity, and download logs.
  • We will suspend your account and access to all purchased content while the dispute is open, and may close it permanently if the dispute is decided in our favor or is found to be abusive.
  • You will not be eligible for future refunds, trials, or discounts.

6. Abuse and fraud

We may deny a refund, cancel access, and refuse future purchases when we reasonably believe someone is abusing this policy. Examples include creating multiple accounts to get repeated trials or refunds, downloading large amounts of content and then asking for a refund, sharing logins, or reselling our content.

7. Exceptions and the law

If we are ever unable to provide a product or service you paid for, or if we end your subscription for a reason other than a breach of our Terms, we will refund any prepaid, unused amount.

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under applicable consumer protection law that cannot be waived. Any refund we give beyond this policy is a one-time courtesy and does not change the policy for future purchases.

We may update this policy. Changes apply to purchases made after the new version is posted.
